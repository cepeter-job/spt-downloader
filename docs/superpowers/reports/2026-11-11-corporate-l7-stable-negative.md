## Two independent capabilities

`projection.kind=corporate-l7-v1` retains its exact two-pin nonnegative contract,
including existing literal Normal/amendment and query-free/View alternatives.
The pure helper learning a negative calculation does not authorize legacy v1
negative packets. Missing optional proof, missing VENDOR, expired history or a
late unavailable history RPC cannot disable a valid nonnegative v1 projection.
Present malformed, conflicting or unbound facts remain fatal custody errors.

The enabled L7 binding can additionally offer the closed sibling
`raw_negative_projection.kind=corporate-l7-stable-negative-v1`. It requires
positive lowercase 64-hex approved and observed CORPORATE/MAIN/VENDOR pins,
actual connected script or script-initiated provider evidence, id-ID,
Asia/Jakarta, original enabled tab/panel boundaries, exact L7 structure and the
selected submitted `?view=true` route. Fetch/preload-only providers do not
qualify. MAIN retains its existing 5MiB reader; the other readers retain 4MiB.
No new endpoint, pin refresh, preload, forced provider fetch or request admission
is introduced. Hashing remains the existing bounded loaded-asset measurement.

Negative admission additionally requires:

- Ordinary selected ICT_RCIT/ICT_RCITR, literal Normal, SUBMITTED/NORMAL and
  original current TaxType ICT_RCIT/current main/current L7 objects.
- Present literal-null Payload PreviousFormData and LastSubmittedFormData;
  missing keys and encoded `"null"` are different and unsupported here.
- Outer IsCarriedForward **absent**, numeric-zero current IsIncomeUnder,
  present literal-null IsIncomeSolely, numeric-one current IsCarriedForward and
  original numeric main TaxYear matched to numeric outer PeriodYear.
- Safe exact negative main and a FIRST current-year row whose original nonzero
  profit already equals that main. A later row cannot rescue a wrong/zero
  first match. Preserve every duplicate, literal year, profit and source leaf.
- Every exact operand, ordered 15-digit intermediate bound, all six reductions
  and every captured-total reconciliation. No clamp, invented zero, dropped leaf
  or replacement footer is allowed.

The identity rule is `submitted-stable-negative-main/raw-profit-identity`.
Payload predicates retain container `['Payload']`; current operands retain
`['Payload','CurrentFormData']`. Original numeric lexemes are not normalized.
Outer carry absence is a separate `presence_facts` item with `present:false`,
not a raw null leaf or a value. Reports name the capability actually consumed.

## Capture-owned continuous child custody

`corporate_l7_lifetime` is optional **manifest-only** metadata, with closed exact
keys and literal-True fresh_root/fresh_l7/stable_root/stable_form/stable_grid/
stable_table/enabled_boundaries facts, bound to attempt, record and literal
source/detail URLs. It always depends on the independently validated actual
component_lifetime; neither a response descriptor nor an observed binding may
supply or override it.

The independent L7 MutationObserver is armed before the inspected View, with no
old corporate root or L7 host anywhere, hidden or visible. It retains the first
unique root/form/grid/p-table/table and at most 64 ancestors. Removals, wrapper
removals, reparenting, replacement and transient hidden duplicates are sticky;
returning to the same final DOM cannot restore permission. Ordinary HTML
wrappers and the existing p-tabview/p-tabpanel/ui-grid-offline framework outlets
may be transparent; foreign business components cannot lend ownership. There
is no requirement to invent an ui-grid-offline host.

The budget is 4096 relevant structural records/candidates, **not all DOM
mutations or row nodes**. Unrelated L3 row/text churn spends no L7 budget.
Unclassifiable detached intermediary ownership or exhausted history removes
only negative permission. Known retained-node contradictions outrank optional
unavailability. This is bounded retained state/relevant work, not a hard CPU/RAM
cap on the browser's mutation queue or selector engine.

Bind original visible/enabled nodes at both L7 boundaries. Final sealing checks
connection, ownership and enabled control binding without requiring L7 to remain
the active panel during the later tab tour. Drain samples again inside the
existing late-response fence; an earlier candidate is never persisted as final
authorization. Stop and explicit safety failures propagate, including when
acquisition itself returns a handle. Cleanup releases observers and handles.

Both live and held readers validate the fact, require the real component fact,
reject descriptor overrides and inject one consistent fact into all packets.
Packet fingerprints include absence, before unsupported fallback. Private,
published and reexported evidence retain the original manifest/response bytes;
replay uses neither a browser nor a new clock.
