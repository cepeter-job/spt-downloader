# Corporate L8 native admission design

## Intent and execution boundary

Continue the approved JSON program by connecting the reviewed L8 pure projection
only when the captured source is provably the same value the inspected submitted
Rupiah form presents. This design is a source-proof deliverable first. It does
not itself enable a form, observer, transport permission or COMPLETE status.

Baseline: main `1d3999f026d4ed82ad6c31eff9272ecb4540717d`, including PR115's
literal L7 annual headers and bounded list restoration. Preserve its committed
navigation/request custody, terminal error classification, Stop propagation and
test/dependency contracts. PR113/114 supply the pure L8 helper and its evidence:
[pure plan](../plans/2026-10-24-corporate-l8-pure.md),
[final report](../reports/2026-10-24-corporate-l8-pure.md).

The user approved autonomous continuation through design, implementation when
proved, runner verification and review. No additional real taxpayer scope,
business-DOM reads or authentication/financial request authority is implied.
Product imports, tests, browser fixtures, builds and EXE execution remain
GitHub-runner-only. Local text/AST/hash inspection is source analysis only.

## Approaches and selected direction

1. Enable the existing projector directly from the original payload. Rejected:
   main inputs can be recalculated and dependency callbacks can replace them.
2. Wait for a fixed quiet interval or inspect Angular/component business state.
   Rejected: a delay cannot prove completion; native observers cannot read that
   business state, and source-rate references may remain pending or fail.
3. Require original captured inputs to be fixed points of every relevant source
   writer, with fresh owned component/route evidence and exact source pins.
   Selected implementation direction, conditional on closing the proof gates
   below. Known contradictions remain fatal; safely unsupported cases fall back
   as a whole record. No partial JSON/DOM mixing.

## Candidate source subset

These are proposed admission conditions, not a declaration of implemented
support. Every ordinary pure `project_l8` restriction remains in force:
matching original numeric literal main/outer years 2020..2100, exact Rupiah
headers/spans, nonnegative safe integral operands, turnover at most 4.8 billion,
research zero and exact captured share/tax/total reconciliation. Preserve source
tokens and provenance; never repair a mismatch with a computed value.

Additionally require:

- Outer and current main `TaxRate` are exactly `TX03`. Both original annual
  month bounds are numeric literal 1 and 12; original main year/period inputs
  agree with the outer inputs that overwrite them during initialization.
- Explicit null `PreviousFormData` and `LastSubmittedFormData` for this initial
  subset. Missing, malformed or differently encoded parents are unsupported;
  known source identity/encoding contradictions retain the existing fatal rules.
- Both outer and current main have explicit numeric literal zero for
  `IsCarriedForward`, `IsNetIncomeReduction`, `IsInvestmentOtherIncome`,
  `IsGrossIncomeVocational` and `IsGrossIncomeResearch`. Missing values,
  strings, booleans and other numeric encodings do not qualify by coercion.
- Current dependent roots `CalculationOfFiscalLossCompensation`,
  `L13AListOfTaxFacilityInInvestment` and `L13BListOfAdditionalGrossIncomeDeduction` are
  absent. Explicit null roots retain the existing unmapped-subtree fallback;
  this first slice does not change generic source-coverage accounting.
  Freshness and source control-flow proof must independently
  establish that their callback caches have never been filled; absence alone
  is insufficient. Supporting captured null dependency roots later requires a
  separately reviewed original-evidence destination, not a global null exemption.
- Original main `NetIncomeBeforeTax` is a nonnegative safe integral operand,
  `FiscalNetIncomeAfterTax` equals it, and `TotalNetIncomeReduction`,
  `TotalGrossIncomeVocational`, `TotalCarriedForward` and
  `TotalGrossIncomeResearch` are zero. Original main `TaxableIncome` equals
  the nonnegative thousand-truncation of that net amount. Refuse signed zero,
  fractional or unsafe inputs. Retain every contributing raw path in provenance.

Keep the ordinary selected Normal/Amendment model gates; never combine their
workbooks. This subset does not admit earlier years, USD, turnover above 4.8
billion, deductions, research, carried losses or populated predecessor inputs.

The main numeric fixed point was independently reconciled with its actual
binary64 implementation. For integral net below 10^15, net/1000 is below 10^12;
the maximum half-ULP is at most 2^-14, below the 0.001 distance of a nonmultiple
of 1000 from an integer. Exact multiples divide to representable integers, and
floor times 1000 remains an exactly representable integer within the bound.
This is separate from the pure helper's existing 11% proof.

Initial main raw patching runs in ngOnChanges before ngOnInit attaches value
subscriptions. Later qualified writers preserve the candidate fixed point.
The L8 child initializes synchronously through attach, changeValue, constants
and setValue(setFormData(datas[0])). Its temporary turnover zero and the captured
turnover both choose the full-facility branch. The final synchronous setValue
restores the original captured turnover, shares, taxes and reconciled sum.

The natural parent PERIOD and valid-date lookups are POST operations in the
pinned application. This design authorizes no added request, replay, catalog
fetch or cache read. A complete amount-invariance proof must cover successful,
pending, rejected and out-of-order parent completions. Existing transport,
privacy, late-response and authorization rules remain active.

## Freshness evidence: implementation prerequisite

Existing `corporate-submitted-spa-v1` retains a Document, one selected route
transition and sampled visible owners. It cannot by itself exclude an initially
hidden corporate owner, root replacement between samples, or a detached cached
component later reattached with populated dependency callbacks.

Candidate stronger evidence is optional, manifest-owned and attempt-bound:
start before the inspected View with no corporate root anywhere in the Document;
retain the exact newly observed corporate root and structural history until
sealing. Inspect only tag/ownership/connectivity facts. Record any L7/L13-A/L13-B
component appearance, including transient inserted/removed nodes, and reject
replacement/duplicate root ownership. Any observed corporate-root removal or
disconnection is fatal, including removal/reinsertion of the same element.
Drain MutationObserver records before
each final sample and inside the existing response-drain fence. No business
input values, checked state, body/footer values or Angular instances may be read.

The pinned router/provider graph independently excludes ordinary cross-route
detached reuse: main imports router module 18293 at 2178; vendor exports Bz as
Al at 2818911. Main Pn imports Tt.Bz.forRoot(pn) without options at 226761, and
root Gt imports Pn. Submitted-list route 218842 and corporate lazy route 215830
are distinct; corporate registers its sole N1 route at 2936584. Vendor default
strategy 2863129 refuses detach/attach and reuses only identical route configs;
constructor 2865714 installs it. Its optional replacement dependency at 2892558
is an inline anonymous class token with no external binding/export. The inspected
main/corporate assets have no strategy override or alternate N1 creation path.
Outlet deactivation 2843341 destroys; activation 2843500 creates the component.
This source proof and the stronger structural history are both required.

Independent source/design review closed the candidate writer, rate, numeric and
router obligations with these corrections. Native L8 remains disabled until
the stronger owned-history and adapter are implemented and runner-verified.
Both the existing corporate-submitted-spa-v1 proof and the stronger proof are
required. Missing historical evidence in old captures remains
unsupported and cannot be synthesized during replay.

## Proposed integration, conditional on proof

Use the existing worker modules and 45-file allowlist. Add only one named L8
native binding and one projection adapter after the proof gates close. The
observer uses one bounded owned-panel wait, exactly one grid/table, exact
headers/footer spans/group/ancestor chain and Stop checks. It reads no business
values. The adapter validates original parent/main inputs, lifetime and source
custody, then calls the unchanged pure helper and binds its provenance to the
actual source response/container/path, keeping outer PeriodYear primary.

Add optional stronger lifetime metadata to manifests with strict replay
validation and cross-packet equality. No renderer/template, publication hierarchy,
JSON byte custody, resource, dependency, workflow, authentication or financial
permission changes are part of this slice. Update AGENTS and architecture for
the supported boundaries only when implementation changes them.
